About InnovateHer

Making space for every voice in tech.

InnovateHer is a women-centric hackathon hosted at Purdue University. Students come to learn, build, and innovate together, with mentors, workshops, and a community that makes it easy to start.

STEM still struggles with representation. In the United States, women earn only 20% of computer science and engineering undergraduate degrees. Technology shapes healthcare, finance, and education, and when the people building it don't reflect the people it serves, innovation is limited.

We're changing that, one weekend of building at a time.

600+participants
100+projects built
200+mentors and organizers
75%women and gender minorities

Who comes aboard

  • Computer science, data science, AI
  • Cybersecurity
  • Computer and other engineering
  • Business analytics, economics, finance
  • UX design, web development
  • Applied math and statistics
  • Psychology, biology, physics

55% of our hackers are first-timers, so nobody starts alone.

Stop 2London

Tracks

Pick a route for your project. Each track is its own destination, with a theme to build around.

Gate A

Financial District

This track encourages participants to build tools that promote financial literacy and economic accessibility as people navigate financial decisions in urban environments. Projects can address the rising cost of city living, housing affordability, and the financial challenges of accessing essential services, helping people make informed decisions regardless of their financial background.

1
Gate B

Reset Route

This track encourages participants to create tech solutions that support emotional well-being and mental health awareness with a focus on underserved communities who often lack access to affordable mental health resources or a sense of belonging in a new place.

2
Gate C

Traveling Tools

This track encourages participants to develop solutions that make the travelling process less overwhelming and safer. These tools can help travelers be more organized or more aware of the places they are visiting.

3
Gate D

Nature’s Love

This track encourages participants to develop solutions to environmental challenges in many places. Projects should prioritize helping alleviate the high environmental impacts that urban areas tend to have or help underserved communities that face most of the impacts of these environmental issues.

4
Gate E

Culture Jam

Put your own spin on the hackathon theme by creating games, interactive experiences, or other user-centered projects that spotlight cultures and customs across the globe! Explore architecture, art, history, social dynamics, or entirely new worlds and futures. This track encourages participants to draw inspiration from places and communities around the world and unleash their creativity!
